The Problem

Tired Of Renovation Projects That Fall Apart Between Contractors?

Late trades, missed handover dates, and a project manager who disappears once the deposit clears. Every business owner or facilities manager in Singapore has felt this. Coordinating separate vendors for demolition, architecture, steel structure, masonry and MEP turns one renovation into five headaches.

Missed Deadlines

Disjointed trades mean no one is accountable for the finish date, and your business pays for every extra day closed.

No Single Point Of Contact

When something goes wrong, every vendor blames the next. You end up project managing the project managers.

Compliance Left To Chance

Landlord reinstatement clauses, WSH requirements and authority approvals get overlooked until they become costly surprises.

Solution

One Contractor. Every Trade.
One Person Accountable.

We built Bloomington Interior around a simple idea: a business owner should be able to call one number and have their entire renovation, from demolition to final cleaning, handled by a team that answers to them directly.

We manage the full scope in-house, including site survey, demolition, architecture, steel structure, masonry, MEP, and handover cleaning, so your project moves on one schedule instead of five.

FAQs

Common Questions About Renovating With Us

What areas of Singapore do you serve?+
We are based in Geylang and take on commercial, industrial, office, retail and institutional renovation projects island-wide across Singapore.
Do you handle both the design and the construction?+
Yes. Our Design & Build Renovation service covers the full scope in-house, from site survey and planning through demolition, architecture, steel structure, masonry, MEP, and handover cleaning.
Can you handle reinstatement works when my lease ends?+
Yes. Our Reinstatement Works service restores leased premises back to landlord requirements, including removal of fittings, ceiling and flooring reinstatement, electrical removal, painting, make-good works and final cleaning.
Do you offer support after the renovation is complete?+
Yes. Our Maintenance Term Contract covers ongoing builder, electrical, ACMV and plumbing maintenance, plus preventive, corrective and emergency response support for commercial and industrial facilities.
