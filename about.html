About Bloomington Interior

We are a Singapore-based design and build renovation contractor, providing full end-to-end commitment and coverage from first site survey to final handover, and every trade in between.

Our Story

Built To Be The One Contractor You Never Have To Chase

We founded Bloomington Interior Pte Ltd on a straightforward frustration shared by most business owners renovating a commercial space: too many contractors, not enough accountability. Every extra vendor is another handoff, another delay, another person to chase for updates.

We built our company around doing the opposite: keeping site survey, demolition, architecture, steel structure, masonry, MEP, and handover cleaning under one roof, coordinated by a single project lead who answers to you directly, from Design & Build fit-outs to Addition & Alteration works, Reinstatement at lease-end, and Maintenance Term Contracts after you've moved in.

We are headquartered at Blk 1013 Geylang East Avenue 3 and take on projects island-wide across Singapore, serving commercial, industrial, office, retail and institutional clients.
