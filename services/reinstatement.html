What Does Our Reinstatement
Service Include?

Our Reinstatement service restores leased premises to the condition your tenancy agreement requires, from fittings removal to final cleaning, run to your lease-end date.

Lease-end reinstatement has one measure of success: does the landlord sign off. We scope your original tenancy condition against the current fit-out before anything is stripped out, so the removal and make-good works pass inspection without back-and-forth.

Scope Of Works

What's Covered Under Reinstatement

Removal Of Fittings

Stripping out partitions, signage and tenant fixtures added during occupancy.

Ceiling Reinstatement

Restoring ceiling finishes to base-building or tenancy agreement condition.

Flooring Reinstatement

Removing tenant flooring and reinstating the original floor finish.

Electrical Removal

Taking out tenant wiring, points and fittings installed during the lease.

Painting

Repainting walls and ceilings to a clean, landlord-ready finish.

Make Good Works

Repairing any damage or alterations made during the tenancy.

Final Cleaning

A full clean ahead of the landlord's inspection and handover.
