What Does Our A&A
Service Include?

Our A&A service covers structural and layout changes to existing buildings, from space reconfiguration to compliance upgrades, built by our in-house renovation team.

Whether you're reconfiguring a floor plate, extending a structure, or upgrading an interior to meet new compliance requirements, we scope the structural, authority and finishing works together from the start, so changes are built once, correctly, by one team instead of separate handoffs.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About A&A In Singapore

Do you handle authority submissions for structural changes?+
We scope compliance and authority requirements as part of your A&A project plan and coordinate the necessary works; specific submissions are confirmed with you during the site survey.
Can A&A works be combined with a full renovation?+
Yes. A&A is often run alongside our Design & Build scope so structural changes and finishing works are planned on one schedule.
How do I know if my project needs A&A instead of standard renovation?+
If your project involves changing layout, structure, or extending the existing footprint, it typically falls under A&A. Call us and describe the change. We'll confirm the right scope.
